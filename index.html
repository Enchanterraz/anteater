How to play

01

Phase 1 is free. Open to everyone, 1 per wallet. Then 10 game phases, then a public phase for leftovers.

02

Pick 1 of 5 ants in the 5-min window (300 wallets max each). The giant anteater eats four — last ant standing wins.

03

Survive & mint. Backers of the winning ant mint for 3 min. Each phase +0.00005 ETH; public phase clears the rest.
